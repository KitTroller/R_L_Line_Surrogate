# exp01 -- the 2x2 comb test on Stage A. 4 cells x 4 seeds = 16 jobs, all on one GPU type.
#
# The trunk's Fourier comb is max_freq*k/F, k = 1..F, so its LOWEST feature is also its
# SPACING (max_freq/F) and its TOP is max_freq. The PLL found the lowest feature controls
# and the top is irrelevant (PLL notes F51, F56). Stage A's current has 99.99% of its power
# below 339 rad/s and ~4e-6 pu above 628 (graphs/dft_line_stage_A.png), so:
#
#     prediction if the BOTTOM matters:  lowest 314 beats lowest 157, the top does not matter
#     prediction if the TOP matters:     top 6283 beats top 628
#
#                     top 628          top 6283
#     lowest 157      F=4,  mf=628     F=40, mf=6283
#     lowest 314      F=2,  mf=628     F=20, mf=6283
#
# Judge on assisted_error_rms (per window). The chained number is hurt at high F by the
# t = S*dt handover (Notes F16) until the shared-boundary eval sets exist.
#
# The F=4 cell is rerun here even though laptop runs exist: those were MPS, these are CUDA,
# and seeds from different devices are different draws. Everything lands in its own
# results_dir so it cannot overwrite a laptop record with the same tag.
#
